# Review

## Creative calls

- Built the film around a single anti-feed journey: the worm follows a path from Topics into Digital Garden, the verified topic counts, and the generated Resource shelf.
- Reused the site's real Dawn palette, Departure Mono and Dank Mono typography, worm asset, topic language, and resource data instead of presenting a generic dashboard.
- Used hard scene cuts with connected spatial ideas: traveling worm → selected topic card → expanding topic panel → shelf → resolved worm line.
- Did not build a narrated walkthrough, reproduce full browser chrome, or show the publishing plugin; the film stays focused on the visitor-facing payoff.

## Verification

- Hyperframes strict check passes with zero lint, runtime, layout, motion, contrast, or craft findings; 57 sampled text checks pass WCAG AA.
- Reviewed the 10-frame beat sheet, four five-frame seam strips, and phone-width contact sheet. The path from idea to topic to resources remains legible at phone scale.
- Tightened the three product-scene entrances after the first review so cuts immediately retain an identifiable heading or panel instead of flashing to an empty canvas.
- Audio clips and timing were measured by the renderer. Nobody listened to the final mix in this workflow.

## Remaining judgment

- The topic-panel expansion is the most abstract shot: the empty bordered panel at the cut is intentional continuity from the enlarged Digital Garden card, with its content blooming in immediately after.
- With more time, I would replace the hard cuts with a bespoke shared-element morph between the selected topic card and the topic hero while preserving the current calm pacing.
- No warnings were retained.
