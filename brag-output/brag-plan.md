# Brag Plan: gil.dev Digital Garden

## What is this app?

gil.dev is a personal digital garden that turns published Markdown notes into connected topic paths, automatically gathering the useful external resources mentioned along the way.

## The angle

The antidote to an endless feed is a path with somewhere to go. The video follows the site's worm mascot away from a generic timeline and into the new Digital Garden topic page, where four notes bloom into a browsable shelf of 76 resources across 48 sites.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

The worm draws a curved path across the Rosé Pine Dawn background as the site's own line resolves in two beats: “Follow an idea.” then “Not a timeline.” The second line lands with the first strong music cue.

## Key moments (the middle)

- Recreate the real topic-card grid and select the flower-marked Digital Garden card.
- Reveal the Digital Garden hero with its real 4 Notes / 76 Resources / 48 Sites counters arriving one by one.
- Open the Resource shelf: the featured Maggie Appleton link stays prominent while site groups unfold beneath it.

## Outro / punchline

“Consume less. Create more.” settles above `gil.dev`, with the worm curling into the final period/path marker.

## User flow worth showing

Open Topics → choose Digital Garden → scan connected notes and expand a site group in the automatically gathered Resource shelf.

## Tone

- Preset: default
- Creative direction: calm anti-feed product film with a playful garden mascot
- Interpretation: Warm and confident rather than frantic; clean UI motion follows the worm's path, with enough hold time to read every line and small tactile accents when real interface elements arrive.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)

- Background: `#faf4ed`
- Accent: pine `#286983`, supported by foam `#56949f`, iris `#907aa9`, gold `#ea9d34`, and love `#b4637a`
- Text: `#464261`
- Display font: Departure Mono
- Body font: Dank Mono
- Strongest visual element: The pink worm guiding viewers through bordered, softly tinted garden cards and the Digital Garden topic's stat-and-resource layout.

## Share copy (draft)

I gave my digital garden a better path: connected notes now grow their own browsable resource shelf—because ideas deserve better than a timeline. 🌱

## Audio direction

- Role: warm rhythmic bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`; bright, curious, and lightly propulsive
- Music treatment: Start at 0:00 under the worm path, keep the bed comfortably below UI sounds, and fade cleanly through the final second.
- Music cue guidance: bundled 109.96 BPM preset; target strong cues near 8.74s for the topic hero, 13.11s for the resource shelf, and 17.47s for the outro turn. Use the 9.29–12.55s beat grid for stat reveals and the 13.64–15.84s grid for site cards, skipping beats as needed to preserve readable holds.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; allow the hero glow and worm-path highlight to breathe slightly with music energy, never as a visible waveform.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched, and soft—one path-draw texture, light card arrivals, one disclosure click, and a gentle outro resolve.
- Audio-coupled moments: worm path draw, Digital Garden card selection, three stat counters, resource-group expansion, final line landing.
- Restraint rule: No constant clicks, cartoon garden noises, or SFX louder than the music bed.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Leave the feed — 3 seconds

Rosé Pine Dawn background with faint radial color blooms. The real pink worm enters from the left and draws a pine path. “Follow an idea.” appears first; “Not a timeline.” replaces the implied feed and holds clearly.

Sequential/interaction: yes — worm path first, then the two short text beats with at least 1.2 seconds of settled headline time.
Audio intent: Invite attention with warmth and one confident landing.
Audio-coupled idea: Path draw gets a soft textured sweep; the second line lands near a beat.
Music: Bright, curious opening from volume 12.
Transition mood: clean path-following wipe → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Choose a garden path — 4 seconds

Recreate the real Topics grid with cards for AI, Astro, Community, Dev Tools, and Digital Garden. The flower-marked Digital Garden card lifts while the others settle back; its real description remains legible.

Sequential/interaction: yes — cards arrive in a gentle stagger, then a cursor/path marker selects Digital Garden.
Audio intent: Tactile discovery without feeling like a dashboard demo.
Audio-coupled idea: Soft card arrivals and one restrained selection click.
Music: Continue the warm bed.
Transition mood: smooth card expansion → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Notes become a map — 5 seconds

The selected card expands into the real Digital Garden hero. Show the flower marker, “Garden path,” and the description “Notes connected by ideas, tended over time…” Then reveal the real counters: 4 Notes, 76 Resources, 48 Sites. Hold the complete set long enough to read.

Sequential/interaction: yes — hero settles, then all three counters arrive one by one across alternating beats; the full set holds for at least 1.5 seconds.
Audio intent: This is the proof moment; let the scale feel satisfying, not boastful.
Audio-coupled idea: Three muted counter ticks aligned within the 9.29–12.55s beat window.
Music: Slightly lift presence into the 8.74s strong cue.
Transition mood: soft downward glide → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The resource shelf grows itself — 5 seconds

Recreate the Resource shelf heading, the featured “A Brief History and Ethos of the Digital Garden” card, and several authentic grouped site cards such as YouTube, GitHub, Wikipedia, and Maggie Appleton. Simulate opening YouTube to reveal two descriptive link rows.

Sequential/interaction: yes — featured card first, site groups next, then one disclosure opens and holds.
Audio intent: Make automation feel quietly magical.
Audio-coupled idea: Cards land on spaced beats; disclosure uses one soft click and a short paper-like reveal.
Music: Continue, with the shelf reveal near 13.11s.
Transition mood: gentle zoom-out and crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Grow something worth returning to — 3 seconds

The UI recedes into a small garden of connected cards. Large text: “Consume less. Create more.” Then `gil.dev` appears beneath it while the worm curls beside the wordmark. Bake this settled composition as the poster frame and frame 0.

Sequential/interaction: yes — statement, then site name and worm resolve; all remain visible through the fade.
Audio intent: Warm resolution with a memorable final thought.
Audio-coupled idea: Final statement lands near 17.47s; a soft tonal resolve accompanies the wordmark.
Music: Fade through 20.0s.
Transition mood: soft final hold

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat, warm, and curious
**Audio summary:** A light rhythmic bed follows the worm through the interface, with only a few soft UI accents reinforcing the real product flow.
